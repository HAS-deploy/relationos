RelationOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15

RelationOS is a private personal CRM. It runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

None of your relationship data. RelationOS does not collect, transmit, or store your contacts, notes, reminders, or tags on our servers — we do not operate any servers.

What lives on your device

All of your contacts, notes, reminders, tags, and settings are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also wipe everything in Settings → Privacy → "Delete all data".

Network activity

The only network activity RelationOS performs is with Apple's App Store for the in-app subscription, and the anonymous-analytics activity described below.

Notifications

If you set a reminder or subscribe to RelationOS Pro (which schedules a daily reconnect notification at 8 AM), RelationOS uses iOS's local notification system. Nothing about the reminders or notifications is sent to us. You can disable them at any time in Settings or in iOS Settings → RelationOS.

Anonymous usage analytics

RelationOS uses PostHog to see which features are useful and which need work. We collect anonymous events — install and foreground, explicit screen views, paywall interactions, and crash / diagnostic metadata — tied to a random per-install identifier. We never collect your name, email, Apple ID, or any of your contacts, notes, reminders, or tags. On-device Apple Intelligence features (note briefs, email digest) run locally and are not sent to PostHog or used for training. You can turn analytics off in Settings. No ads. No App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Children

RelationOS is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
