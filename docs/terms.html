The app

RelationOS is a personal CRM that lives on your phone. Your contacts, notes, and reminders are stored on your iPhone. The app collects anonymous product analytics (PostHog) to help us improve it — see the Privacy Policy for the details and your contact data is never included.

What's in v1.1:

  • Add contacts manually, or import them from your iPhone Contacts (pick selectively, or in bulk with permission) or from a vCard.
  • Daily Reconnect list — five people every morning who are slipping away.
  • Cooling relationships highlighted in the Daily Reconnect list.
  • Quick-log Call / Text / Email — opens Apple's standard composers and records what you sent. iOS doesn't share call or text history with third-party apps, so the log is the substitute.
  • Reminders — local notifications on your iPhone. No server, no calendar dependency.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen rectangular widgets surface the Daily Reconnect list.
