Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Your contacts, notes, and reminders are stored on your iPhone. The app collects anonymous product analytics (PostHog) — feature-use events with no contact data attached — to help us improve it. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
How does the 14-day free Pro period work?
Every fresh install of RelationOS unlocks full Pro for 14 days automatically — no card, no commitment, no Subscribe tap required. After day 14 the app reverts to the free tier (up to 100 contacts) unless you subscribe.
How much does RelationOS Pro cost?
$11.99 per month or $89.99 per year (about 37% off vs. monthly).
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Open iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → RelationOS Pro → Cancel Subscription. The current billing period continues; no further charges after that.
What happens to my data if I cancel or the trial ends?
Nothing is lost. Your contacts, notes, and reminders stay on your phone. You return to the free tier (limit: 100 contacts).
How do I delete all my data?
In RelationOS, go to Settings → Privacy → Delete all data → confirm. Your contacts, notes, and reminders are wiped. The app returns to a fresh-install state.
How do I import my iPhone contacts?
Tap the + button in Contacts → Import. Choose "Pick from Phone" to hand-select a few (no permission prompt), "Import all from Phone" for the full address book with a confirmation step, or "Import vCard" if a contact card was emailed to you.
Why doesn't RelationOS show my call or text history?
iOS doesn't share SMS, iMessage, call history, or email content with third-party apps. The "Log call, text, or email" buttons in each contact open Apple's standard composers and record what you sent — that's the closest substitute the platform allows.
