The app

RelationOS is a personal CRM that lives entirely on your phone. Most relationship apps want your contacts uploaded to their servers. RelationOS doesn't. Your people, your notes, your reminders — all of it stays on your iPhone. No account. No cloud sync by default. No data leaves your device unless you turn it on. WHAT IT DOES • Remember everyone — Add contacts, notes, and context as you meet people. Search the way you think: "Who do I know in Austin?", "Who introduced me to Sarah?" • Daily reconnect list — Five people every morning who are slipping away. Built from how often you actually talk, not how often LinkedIn pings them. • Smart reminders — "Follow up in 2 weeks", "Next time I'm in Austin", "Before her birthday." Reminders that know context, not just dates. • Relationship decay detection — See which connections are cooling before they go cold. Get gentle nudges to reach out. • Notes that survive — Capture the small things people share with you. Their kid's name. The book they recommended. The conflict they're working through. RelationOS surfaces them next time it matters. • iOS-native — Widgets, App Intents, Shortcuts, Lock Screen integration. Works the way iOS works.
